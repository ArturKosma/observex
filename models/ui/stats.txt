# observex: where the game's numbers sit in its interface (core/GameStats reads this; tools/ui/make_digits.py cuts the
# glyphs). Screen pixels of the screen it was measured on (2560 x 1440, Lineage 2 Reborn, the user's UI scale), on the
# pieces ui.txt finds (the status window, the target window, the cast bar, the hotbar). The lines read are shown in
# their order (the gauges of core/GameStats).
#
# glyphs NAME CHARS PITCH W H [yellow]
#   A font of fixed width: one W x H picture per character of CHARS, in that order, in NAME.pgm (their grey pixels,
#   side by side) and NAME-mask.pgm (255 on the letter and the dark line round it, 0 where the bar shows through, so
#   the bar's colour never counts). Each next character starts PITCH pixels on. "yellow": yellow letters over any
#   picture (a cooldown's seconds), found by colour: NAME.pgm is the share of samples each pixel was yellow on.
#   Indented "from IMAGE X Y TEXT" lines are for make_digits.py: TEXT is written on IMAGE (a recorded image, from the
#   project folder, or a clip's frame: clip.mp4@FRAME) with its first character's picture at X Y; the pictures are the
#   mean over all of them.
# bar NAME PIECE FILL TOP BOTTOM LEFT RIGHT [text GLYPHS ROW FROM] [empty COLOUR]
#   A bar in piece PIECE: rows TOP to just before BOTTOM, columns LEFT to just before RIGHT, from the piece's top left
#   (a RIGHT below 1 from its right side, as the window may be resized). FILL is the colour of its filled part (red,
#   blue or lilac); how full it is = where the first run of that colour from LEFT ends. "text" if a number is written
#   on it in GLYPHS: the pictures' top row is ROW (a pixel up or down is looked at too), anywhere from column FROM on.
#   "empty": the colour of its empty part past the fill's end (red, blue or grey: dark red, dark blue, grey; else
#   dark). Where it is not that colour a window is over the bar, and how full it is is not said.
# cast PIECE TOP BOTTOM LEFT RIGHT
#   The cast bar (gauge "Casting"): how far its light blue fill's white head is, from column LEFT (0) to RIGHT (1), on
#   rows TOP to just before BOTTOM; what is cast is the added skill whose cooldown began last.
# skills PIECE GLYPHS
#   The skills the user added (models/ui/skills/NAME.pgm, from the Stats tile's Add Skill), one gauge each, looked for
#   among the miniatures in every piece PIECE: how much of the cooldown is left, and its seconds in GLYPHS (yellow).
# words NAME PIECE TOP BOTTOM LEFT RIGHT
#   White words on piece PIECE (gauge NAME), read by Windows' OCR once per new picture of them: the widest run of
#   white in rows TOP to just before BOTTOM, columns LEFT to just before RIGHT (below 1: from the piece's right).
# close PIECE TOP BOTTOM LEFT RIGHT
#   The window's close button (a light X) on piece PIECE, rows TOP to just before BOTTOM, columns LEFT to just before
#   RIGHT from the piece's right side (a few pixels either way are looked at). While it is not there, another window
#   covers the piece's right end (the side found is that window's): its bars and words are not read.
